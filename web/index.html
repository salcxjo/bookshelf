    Import from Kobo

    Connect your Kobo by USB and choose KoboReader.sqlite from its hidden .kobo folder. You’ll see a preview first, and nothing changes until you press Import. Books you already have are only moved forward (never from Read back to Reading), and your own dates and ratings are kept.

    Import from Goodreads

    On Goodreads open My Books, then Import and export, and export your library. Choose the CSV file here. You’ll see a preview first, and nothing changes until you press Import. Books you already have are only moved forward (never from Read back to Reading); your own ratings, dates and notes are kept, and only blanks are filled in. Just the main author of each book is imported.
